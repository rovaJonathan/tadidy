    Welcome to Tadidy

    Tadidy remembers everything you copy — text, images, files and links — so you never lose a clip again.

    ⌘⇧V

    Open your history from any app.

    Or click in the menu bar.

    Let Tadidy paste for you

    To paste a clip straight into the app you’re using, Tadidy needs Accessibility access. It only sends ⌘V — it never reads your screen or what you type.

    1. Click Open System Settings.
    2. Turn on Tadidy in Privacy & Security › Accessibility.
    Waiting for permission…

    You can do this later. Until then, clips are copied and you paste them with ⌘V.

    Set up your history

    You can change these anytime in Preferences.

    Pinned clips and snippets don’t count.

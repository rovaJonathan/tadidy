# TAD-032 — Show the popover under the tray icon when the Mac has several displays

> Phase 1 · P0 · core · Discovered in: TAD-025 (manual QA on the signed build: the popover could not be opened to reset the history)

## Context

On the signed universal build, on a MacBook Pro (built-in display) with an external display connected (2560 × 1440), a left click on the tray icon shows nothing. Every click logs `could not place the popover under the tray icon error=No monitor found for the window` (`windows.rs`, `PopoverWindow::reveal`, from `tauri-plugin-positioner`'s `move_window(TrayPosition::TrayBottomCenter)`). `show()` then reports no error, so the window is probably shown where it was, off every visible screen. MNU-02 is the main entry point of the app: with two displays Tadidy is unusable from the tray.

## Scope

- Reproduce with two displays (menu bar on either display, external display left or right of the built-in one, different scale factors) and log the tray rectangle, the window position and the monitors Tauri reports (positions and sizes only).
- Find why the positioner finds no monitor (tray position in another coordinate space or scale factor, a monitor lookup on the window's current position, the plugin's handling of several displays…) and fix it: the popover opens under the icon on the display that holds the menu bar that was clicked.
- When placing fails anyway, never show the window off-screen: fall back to a visible place (e.g. centred on the display under the mouse pointer) and log it.

## Out of scope

- The Quick Panel's position (Preferences › Quick Panel position, DESIGN.md §2).
- The Preferences window's placement (TAD-017).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Given two displays, when the tray icon is clicked on the menu bar of either display, then the popover shows under the icon on that display, focused.

- [ ] Given a single display, then the popover still opens under the icon (no regression).
